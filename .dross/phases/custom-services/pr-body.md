## Decisions locked

- **block_scope** 🔒 — A custom service blocks every mapped child client via one `||domain^$client=...` rule per domain; adults are never blocked. Per-child unblock arrives with permanent-toggles. *(Mirrors 'blocked for the kids' without touching parents' devices; grants punch @@ exceptions on top; avoids pre-building permanent-toggles.)*
- **delete_refs** 🔒 — Delete returns 409 naming the grant while an active grant covers the service; otherwise it deletes and strips the service from buttons, deleting any button left with no services. *(Never silently re-blocks mid-reward; buttons are cheap to clean up automatically.)*
- **client_targeting** 🔒 — Rules target clients as `$client='<persistent client name>'` (quoted, escaped). *(The app already tracks clients by persistent name and handles renames; IP lists would drift.)*
- **marker_format** 🔒 — Managed region is delimited by `! adguard-reward begin` and `! adguard-reward end` comment lines. *(AdGuard `!` comments are inert; a fixed, greppable pair makes the region unambiguous for r-02.)*
- **uniqueness** 🔒 — Custom service names must be unique (case-insensitive) across custom and built-in services, and a domain may belong to only one custom service; conflicts return 409. *(Avoids indistinguishable entries in lists and keeps per-domain rules and ref-counting single-owner.)*
- **custom_icon** 🔒 — Custom services render a letter avatar from their name; no icon upload. *(AdGuard serves no icon for them and an upload path is scope creep.)*
- **management_ui** 🔒 — Custom services are created, edited and deleted in a 'Services' section of Settings next to Buttons. *(Settings already hosts the button editor; keeps the phone home screen unchanged.)*
